The Company

We are an independent energy company focused on identifying and responsibly developing overlooked oil and gas opportunities in mature basins.
We combine technical expertise, commercial discipline and practical infrastructure solutions to progress resources that might otherwise remain undeveloped.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to maximise the economic value of local gas through efficient development, rigorous environmental stewardship and responsible resource management.
Our current project has the potential to create substantial and lasting value for government, the economy and the wider community during the transition to a lower-carbon energy system.
