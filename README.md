# Marque Oil & Gas Ltd – Corporate & Project Website

An independent energy website developed to showcase overlooked oil and gas opportunities in mature basins, highlighting the Isle of Man gas development project, environmental commitments, and long-term economic benefits.

## Project Overview
This website serves as a public-facing informational portal for Marque Oil & Gas Ltd. It features multi-tab project breakdowns, interactive development timelines, comparison data regarding local gas versus renewable alternatives, and dedicated resources/FAQ sections.

## Key Sections & Features
* **The Opportunity:** Overview of the Isle of Man gas discovery, spatial overlap considerations, and resource choices.
* **The Case for Gas:** Strategic advantages covering energy security, price stability, tax revenues, and carbon capture integration.
* **Development Vision:** Step-by-step 5-stage timeline tracking infrastructure progression from Barrow processing to carbon storage.
* **Economic Comparison:** Detailed interactive evaluation criteria comparing local gas development against renewable alternatives.
* **Resources & FAQs:** Public consultation materials, downloadable document cards, and answers to common voter questions.

## Tech Stack
* **HTML5** & **CSS3** (Manually coded layouts utilizing custom CSS variables, flexbox, and grid systems)
* **Vanilla JavaScript** (Tab switching, scroll-to-top functionality, interactive image modals, and scroll-reveal observers)
* **Google Fonts** (Montserrat & Sen typography)
